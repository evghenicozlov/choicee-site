This Privacy Policy explains how Choicee ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android.
By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
We do not require account creation or direct personal information to use the app. We do not store any personal data on our own servers — all data processing is handled by third-party services described below.

1.1 Automatically collected data
• Device information (model, OS version, language)
• App usage data (screens viewed, interactions)
• Approximate location (country/region)
• Advertising identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID)

1.2 Advertising data We use third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) which may collect:
• Advertising ID
• Device identifiers
• IP address

1.3 Purchase data
When you purchase a subscription, transaction information (such as subscription status and purchase date) is processed by Apple or Google. We receive only a confirmation of your subscription status — we do not receive or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing information. Choicee offers an auto-renewable subscription that provides the following benefits:

• Removal of all advertisements
• Access to additional content
• Unlocked premium features
• Unlimited use of the app

All transactions are processed through the official billing system of your platform:

• iOS Apple In-App Purchase — governed by Apple's Privacy Policy
• Android Google Play Billing — governed by Google's Privacy Policy

We do not collect, store, or process your payment information directly. Subscription management (renewal, cancellation) is handled through your device's platform settings.

To cancel a subscription:

• iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
• Android Google Play → Subscriptions The app displays ads using third-party services, including App Open ads, Banner ads, Interstitial ads, and Rewarded ads (full-screen ads). These formats may be shown at different times during app usage.

Advertising partners may use cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies to serve ads (personalized or non-personalized), measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Ads are not shown to users with an active subscription. On Apple devices, we request user permission via App Tracking Transparency (ATT) where required.

If you allow tracking, your device identifier may be used to deliver personalized ads. If you do not allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they may be non-personalized. If you are located in regions that require consent (such as the EEA or UK):

• We request your consent before showing personalized advertising
• You can accept or refuse personalized ads
• Refusing consent will not affect core functionality of the app

You can also change your advertising consent at any time inside the app: Settings → Ads Consent We use collected data to:

iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

You may also reset or limit your advertising ID at any time. We use third-party providers that may process data according to their own privacy policies:
