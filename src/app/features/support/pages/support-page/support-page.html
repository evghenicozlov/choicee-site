We’re here to help you with any questions, issues, or feedback related to the Choicee app.
If you need support, you can contact us directly:

Email: feedback.choicee.app@gmail.com

We usually respond within 24–72 hours. Before contacting support, please check the following:

App not working properly:

• Make sure you are using the latest version of the app
• Restart the app or your device

Ads not loading or too frequent:

• Check your internet connection
• Restart the app
• Ads frequency may vary depending on availability from ad networks

Sound or animation issues:

• Check device mute/silent mode
• Ensure system volume is enabled We appreciate your feedback! If you have ideas for improving the app, new features, or general suggestions, feel free to contact us. The app uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob).

You can manage your advertising preferences inside the app: Settings → Ads Consent If you are reporting a bug, please include:

• Device model
• iOS / Android version
• App version
• Description of the issue
• Screenshots (if possible)

This helps us fix problems faster. For information about how we handle data, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
